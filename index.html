Without being opened, ziai reads and distills your notifications — and asks "Want to see this now?" for the ones that matter.

Notification text and senders never leave your device. Only anonymous labels are sent to improve ziai, and you can turn that off. No sign-up, no login.

The noise is quietly folded away; only what you can't afford to miss remains. From A to Z, ziai proposes the final Z — the conclusion — and asks you to confirm.
